The different kinds of variable bindings in HIR. I.e. A single identifier such as let x = ... or a tuple binding such as let (x, y) = .... In this system, the Ident variant is used as the atomic unit of variable bindings. Tuple bindings are represented as a collection of Ident bindings, referenced by a HirId.
